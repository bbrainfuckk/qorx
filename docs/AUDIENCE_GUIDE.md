# Audience guide

Qorx should be usable by people who want different levels of proof.

## Normal users

Use the monitor, tray, and `qorx doctor`. The useful question is simple: is the
local gateway running, is the workspace indexed, and did the answer cite local
evidence instead of guessing?

## Engineers

Use `qorx --version`, `qorx context snapshot`, `qorx context verify`,
`qorx security attest`, `qorx money`, and `scripts/safer-check.ps1`. Treat every
claim as a checkable report with a boundary field.

## Scientists

Treat Qorx as an instrumented local system. The measurements are local token
estimates, indexed evidence counts, hash checks, and routed-provider counters.
They are useful when the assumptions are shown beside the result.

## Mathematicians

Look for invariants: stable handles for stable inputs, deterministic hashes,
bounded evidence packs, explicit budgets, and rejection of unsupported claims.
The compression language is Baseline-to-Compact accounting over local state, not
a theorem about arbitrary information.

## Physicists

Qorx uses terms such as quark, photon, horizon, and lattice as compact product
vocabulary. These are labels for local context units, carriers, boundaries, and
graphs. They are not physics claims, and Qorx is not a physics engine.

## Shared rule

Do not sell token reduction as truth. Qorx must show cited local evidence,
bounded assumptions, and separate local context avoided from billable provider
savings.
